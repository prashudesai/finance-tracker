# Record search

Use the compact Search button near the bottom of any workspace. It follows the selected Appearance style and sits above Modern/Emerald mobile navigation. The button hides while a mobile form input or a dialog is active, has a 48px tap target, and is omitted from print.

Type at least two characters and optionally choose a record type. Results match names, sources, categories, notes, ISO dates and amounts, including Indian comma/currency formatting. Every search term must match. Results are capped at 40 while showing the full match count.

Personal income, investments, debts, PF, PPF and NPS follow the selected family profile. Household expenses remain shared. Active records only are included; archived accounts are managed in Settings. Queries stay on this device, are not persisted or sent to a server, and reset when closed or signed out.

Open page navigates to the matching workspace. Income/expense results select the relevant financial year, month and containing history page; Emerald Flow also opens the appropriate workspace tab. Search never edits, deletes or saves financial records. Notes are rendered as text.
