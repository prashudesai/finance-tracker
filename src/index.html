Monthly balances

How is the monthly balance calculated?
Calculated balance = Opening balance + recorded income − recorded expenses − Stallion PMS contributions

Deducted: Recorded expenses and Stallion PMS contributions
Tracked separately: NPS, PPF and PF contributions are not deducted in this calculation

Enter a bank balance only once at the start of a month. This is also the closing balance of the previous month. For example, November 1 opening is October 31 closing. When first starting the tracker, older months do not require an opening balance or review. Do not enter the same expenses twice.

Employee plus employer contributions, excluding the EPS portion, are added to PF invested and current once from next month onward, at the month start while the app is open, or on next open if it was closed.
