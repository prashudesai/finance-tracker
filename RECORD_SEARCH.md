# Local record search

Dashboard → Search records opens a read-only search dialog. Type at least two characters and optionally choose a record type. Results match names, sources, categories, notes, ISO dates and numeric amounts, including Indian comma/currency formatting. Multiple words must all match. Results are capped at 40 and show the full match count.

Personal income, investments, debts, PF, PPF and NPS follow the selected family profile. Household expenses remain shared. Active records only are included; archived accounts are managed in Settings. The query is not persisted or sent to a server. It resets when closed or signed out.

Open page navigates to the relevant workspace. Income and expense results select the financial year, month and containing history page; Emerald Flow also opens the appropriate workspace tab. Search does not edit, delete or save financial records. Results render text content, not HTML from notes.
